An ad goes from signed order to live on the kiosk in about 15 minutes of your time. Each ad shows for 8 seconds in turn with the business logo, name, walking time, tagline, website and a QR code.

  1. Pitch. Show the business the kiosk in person, or open the kiosk link on your phone. Leave the pricing page: $399 a year or $60 a month for one location.
  2. Order. The customer orders at the website checkout: city Los Angeles, CA; business and contact details; logo or artwork, or "Design it for me"; then signs the Advertiser Agreement. You can fill it in with them on the spot.
  3. Payment. Once the Stripe links are connected, checkout sends them to pay. Until then, invoice them; the order email says "Invoice the customer".
  4. Order email. It arrives at sarvesh.joshiaz@gmail.com with an order number (CP-…), their details, signature and files attached.
  5. Add the ad. Ads → Add an ad. Fill in business name, what it is, tagline (110 characters), website, QR link (starts with https://) and linked place, which adds the walking time. Upload the logo, set campaign start and end dates, and turn on Show on kiosks.
  6. Record the deal. Open Deal details under the ad: contact, monthly price and status. It stays private and feeds the revenue total on Overview.
  7. Publish. Check the live preview beside the ad, then click Publish to kiosks. The ad is on the lobby screen within about 2 minutes.
  8. Send their report link. Advertiser report → Copy live link, and email it to them. It shows guests reached, views with a guest present and QR scans, updated live, with no sign-in.
  9. Every month. Print or save the report as a PDF for the check-in. Next best actions on Overview flags ads about to end, so you can talk renewal.

What advertisers get

  • Their ad in rotation on the lobby screen, all day, every day.
  • A QR code that opens their website or directions on the guest's phone, with every scan counted.
  • A sponsored stop in "Plan my evening" when they are close by, labeled Sponsored.
  • When they are listed under Places, the AI concierge can recommend them to guests.
  • A live report link and a monthly PDF.

Keeping the screen sellable

Ads rotate in order, so more advertisers means each one comes around less often. With 8 ads each shows about once a minute. Hold the line at 8 per screen and start a waitlist after that.
