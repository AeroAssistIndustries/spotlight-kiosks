How CityPulse worksOur story

Good places deserve to be found.

A few minutes in a lobby or waiting area can become a useful connection.

Visitors might be choosing lunch, looking for directions, exploring a service or learning what a venue offers. CityPulse brings those possibilities together on a professionally managed touch screen.

Our purpose is practical: useful information for visitors, a modern amenity for venues and relevant visibility for the surrounding business community.

From a tap to a network.

People learned to tap, scan and look things up on a screen. CityPulse puts that in the places where they wait.

  1. 2010

    Touch screens become everyday

    Tablets and smartphones teach everyone to tap for answers. A screen stops being something you watch and becomes something you use.

  2. 2015

    Lobbies start to go digital

    Hotels, clinics and dealerships add screens and self-service kiosks. Local businesses nearby are still mostly left off them.

  3. 2020

    QR codes come back

    Menus, check-ins and payments move to the phone in your hand. Scanning a code becomes second nature.

  4. 2022

    The CityPulse idea takes shape

    A digital concierge for hotel lobbies, paid for by the local businesses guests are looking for.

  5. 2025

    The offering comes together

    Kiosk hardware, venue content, simple advertising packages and a no-cost program for host venues.

  6. 2026

    CityPulse Kiosks launches

    A national network for hotels, medical offices, car dealerships, restaurants and other places where people spend time. The first local ads go on screen in October.
