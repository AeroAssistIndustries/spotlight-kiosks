A little clarity before you start.

Is there a cost to host a kiosk?

Qualified venues get the kiosk hardware, software, installation, content updates and maintenance at no cost. It depends on a site review and a signed Host Agreement.

What does my venue need to provide?

A visible, accessible spot with a standard power outlet. We review internet availability and the right installation with your team.

Who looks after the kiosk?

Spotlight manages the equipment, software, content updates and routine maintenance. Contact support for installation or service questions.

Who can advertise?

Local businesses, restaurants and entertainment, medical and wellness services, automotive businesses and retail brands can ask about placements that fit.

What does advertising cost?

Annual placements start at $399. Multi-kiosk plans are priced by market and number of screens, and confirmed in your quote.

What do advertisers see in reports?

Totals: how often your message was shown, how many people opened it and how many took it home by phone. Kiosks don't identify who tapped.
