FAQ

Pricing questions.

Do I have to sign a long contract?

No. Monthly plans run month to month after an initial three months, which is the minimum time for SEO and social to show results. Website projects are a one-time agreement with a 50% deposit.

Can I start with one service?

Yes. Social media, local SEO, reputation and AI search visibility are each available on their own. Most clients start with one and bundle into a plan once they see the reports.

Who owns the website, content and accounts?

You do. Your domain, hosting, WordPress site, social accounts and ad accounts stay in your name. We work inside them as a manager, and we hand everything back cleanly if you ever leave.

What happens if I'm not happy?

Tell us. We'll fix the work first. If it still isn't right, monthly plans can be cancelled with 30 days' notice after the initial term, and you keep everything we've built.

Is there a setup fee?

No setup fee on monthly plans. Website projects take a 50% deposit to start, with the balance due before launch.

What about ad spend?

Paid campaigns run from your own Google or Meta ad account, so you pay the platform directly and keep the account history. Our management fee is included in Pulse Max or quoted separately.
