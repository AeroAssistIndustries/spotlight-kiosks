Ask our experts

FAQs on reputation.

Ask a question

Can you remove bad reviews?

Only reviews that break a platform's rules can be flagged, and we do that. For the rest, a prompt, professional response does more good than most people expect.

Is asking for reviews allowed?

Yes, as long as you ask everyone and don't pay for them. Our requests follow each platform's guidelines.

How do you get customer contact details for requests?

From your booking system, POS, CRM or a simple daily list. We set up the connection once.

What about HIPAA for medical practices?

Requests contain no health information and responses never confirm someone is a patient. We follow a practice-safe playbook.

Will you respond to reviews in my voice?

Yes. We agree a tone and a few house rules up front, and you can review responses before they post if you prefer.
