Ask our experts

FAQs on websites.

Ask a question

How long does a website take?

A launch site takes 2–3 weeks; a business site 3–5 weeks. Custom builds depend on the scope we agree in discovery. The most common delay is content — we help by writing it.

Do I own the website?

Yes. The domain, hosting account and WordPress site are yours. If you ever leave, you take everything with you.

Can you work with my existing site?

Often, yes. We'll audit it first and tell you plainly whether to rebuild or improve what you have.

What does hosting cost?

Good managed WordPress hosting runs $25–$40 a month, billed by the host to you. We'll recommend one and set it up.

Will my old Google rankings survive a rebuild?

Yes, if the redirects and page structure are handled properly — which is part of every rebuild we do.

Do you write the copy?

Yes. Business sites include copywriting for every page, from an interview with you and your existing materials.
