Our approach

How we approach it.

Five moves, in the order they pay off.

  1. 01

    A menu-first website

    Menu, hours, reservations and ordering above the fold, loading in under two seconds. Menu schema so Google shows dishes in search.

  2. 02

    Google profile that sells tonight

    Weekly posts for specials and events, menu and photos kept current, reservation and order links connected.

  3. 03

    Reviews that fill tables

    QR cards on tables and receipts, every review answered within a business day, kitchen feedback summarized weekly.

  4. 04

    Social from your own kitchen

    A monthly shot list for your phone; we edit the reels, write the captions and reply to the DMs.

  5. 05

    Local search and AI answers

    'Best brunch near me' and 'restaurants with patio' are won with content, photos and structured data.

FAQ

Ask our experts.

The questions restaurants & bars ask us most.

Can you connect online ordering and reservations?

Yes — Toast, Square, OpenTable, Resy, Tock, ChowNow and most platforms.

We change our menu often. Can we update it ourselves?

Yes. The menu is editable in WordPress, and we push changes to Google and delivery platforms with the care plan.

Do you shoot food photos?

We direct a monthly shot list for your phone and edit it. For launches we can arrange a professional shoot.

What's the fastest win?

Usually the Google Business Profile: complete it, add real photos, post weekly. Calls and direction requests move within weeks.
