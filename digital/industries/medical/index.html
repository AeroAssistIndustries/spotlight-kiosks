Our approach

How we approach it.

Five moves, in the order they pay off.

FAQ

Ask our experts.

The questions medical & dental ask us most.

Is this HIPAA-compliant?

Our processes are designed to keep protected health information out of marketing: review requests carry no health details, responses never confirm a patient relationship, and we don't use tracking pixels on patient pages.

Can you integrate online booking?

Yes — NexHealth, Zocdoc, Dentrix Ascend, Weave and most practice systems.

How do you handle a negative review from a patient?

A calm, compliant response that invites them to call, never discusses care, and a note to you with the details.

Do you work with multi-provider practices?

Yes. Provider pages, specialty pages and a location structure that ranks for each.
