A drone company's website, rebuilt to win briefings.

AeroAssist builds drone-as-first-responder aircraft for police and fire agencies. We rebuilt aeroassist.us around one action — book a briefing — so agencies arrive at the first call already sold on the platform.

The challenge

Where they started.

AeroAssist's original site explained the technology in depth but never asked for the meeting. Pages were slow on phones, the compliance story agencies care about (American-built, NDAA §848) was buried, and there was no clear path from 'interesting' to 'let's talk'.

The work

What we did.

  • A new site built around a single call to action — Book a briefing — reachable in one tap from every page
  • A platform story in plain English: what launches, what responders see, and what happens in the first 90 seconds of a call
  • Trust marks (American-built, NDAA-compliant, Phoenix-based) placed where an agency buyer looks first
  • Solutions, program and resources pages written for the questions procurement teams actually search
  • Speed and accessibility pass: sub-2-second mobile load, keyboard-friendly navigation
