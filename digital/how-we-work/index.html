How we work

Audit. Plan. Build. Grow.

Every engagement runs the same way, whether you need one service or all of them. Here's what happens, and when.

Start with the audit

The four steps

  1. 01

    Audit

    We review your site, search, reviews and social and tell you plainly what's working and what isn't. Free, in two business days.

  2. 02

    Plan

    One page: what we'll do, what it costs and what you should expect by when. You keep it even if you don't hire us.

  3. 03

    Build

    Website, profiles, listings, content calendar and review flows set up in the first 30 days.

  4. 04

    Grow

    Every month: posts, SEO work, review responses and a report you can read in two minutes.

Timeline

The first 90 days.

  1. Days 1–2Free audit delivered: website, search, reviews, social, with the three fixes that matter most.
  2. Week 1Kickoff call. One-page plan agreed. Access set up through platform manager features, never shared passwords.
  3. Weeks 2–4Build: Google Business Profile completed, listings synced, review requests live, first content calendar approved, website work started.
  4. Month 2Everything running. First monthly report. Early wins usually show in profile views, calls and reviews.
  5. Month 3Strategy review. Rankings and social reach trending. Decide what to double down on.
